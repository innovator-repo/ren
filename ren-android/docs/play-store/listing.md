## Full description (4000)

```
Ren teaches Japanese the way it actually sticks: a few minutes every day, every kanji built from
pieces you already know, and reading real Japanese as soon as you can.

PULL EVERY KANJI APART
Before you meet 休 you learn 亻 (person) and 木 (tree). Then you see the picture: a person resting
against a tree. Over 50 radicals turn 140+ kanji into small stories instead of random strokes.

TYPE IT. DRAW IT. NEVER PICK IT.
No multiple choice. You write kanji stroke by stroke on the canvas, and type readings with a real
12-key flick keyboard. Recall you produce is recall you keep.

A SCHEDULE THAT KNOWS YOU
Ren uses FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition scheduler, to bring each card back right before you
would forget it. Daily goals keep sessions short, and streak freezes cover the days life happens.

STORIES AT YOUR LEVEL
Graded stories, dialogues and real-life texts unlock as you learn. Each one uses words you know
plus a new one or two. Tap any word for its reading, parts and pitch accent, and save whole
sentences to review.

A CLEAR PATH
Kana Sprint, Radical Roots, Foundation Kanji (N5), Intermediate Mastery (N4 to N3). Each stage
opens once the last one is stable, not just seen.

SEE HOW IT CONNECTS
The Matrix maps every kanji to its radicals and words, so you can watch your knowledge grow.

CALM BY DESIGN
No ads. No account. No tracking. Ren works offline and keeps your progress on your phone.

What is inside:
• 92 hiragana and katakana, with romaji off from day 4
• 57 radicals with picture stories
• 143 kanji (N5 and early N4) with stroke order
• 228 words, 13 verb pairs, example sentences
• 10 graded stories and dialogues
• Pitch accent for every word
• Daily reminder, home-screen widget, light and dark mode
```
