# Ren privacy policy

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

Ren is a Japanese study app. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## What Ren stores

- **Your study progress**: which kana, radicals, kanji and words you have learned, your review
  history and schedule, sentences you save from stories, your streak.
- **Your settings**: daily goals, reminder time, appearance, sound and haptics choices.

All of this stays in Ren's private storage on your device.

## What Ren does not do

- Ren has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
- Ren does not request the Internet permission. It cannot send anything to us or anyone else.
- Ren contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Ren's progress
and settings in your device backup and restore them on a new phone. That backup is handled by
Android and Google under your Google account settings; we never receive it. You can turn it off
in your phone's system settings.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | The optional daily reminder, sent only when reviews are waiting. You can turn it off in Ren's settings or in system settings. |
| Vibrate | Haptic feedback while you study. Can be turned off in Ren's settings. |

## Text-to-speech

Ren reads Japanese aloud with your phone's own speech engine (for example, Speech Services by
Google). The text of the word or sentence is handed to that engine on your device. Its handling
is governed by the engine provider's own policy.

## Children

Ren is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall Ren, or clear its storage in system settings, to remove everything it stored.
